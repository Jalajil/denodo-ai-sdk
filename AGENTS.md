# AGENTS.md

## Service terminology

Use the actual names and terminology used by the service in documentation, diagrams, interface text, and explanations. Do not replace established service terms with synonyms or alternative wording.

When clarification is needed, retain the service's term and explain its meaning in plain language. Preserve the exact names of API endpoints, parameters, response fields, and other service identifiers.

When unsure, verify the terminology against the service implementation or official documentation before writing.
